## Freeze 3: 2026-10-27 13:21 EDT

Direction change after Cory Hall's note (see ../DIRECTION.md): the object of study is the generated serializer (A1) and what the SDK's critic loop does to it. Scorer, generator, graphkit and every gold.json are byte-identical to Freeze 2 except score.py, which gains per-pass rows for the new arm and changes nothing in alignment, classification or summaries (the self-test still passes). New since Freeze 2:

- pipeline_critic.py: arm a1_critic (A1+QA). A1 generation, then the server's critic session (start_critic_review, submit_critic_revision, finalize_critic_review) with CRITIC_POLICY disabled (deterministic checks) or manual (the same LLM critiques from the prompt package). Each pass is snapshotted and scored as a1_critic@passN. A crashed serializer gets critic.fix_attempts tracebacks back, at pass 1 too.
- pipeline_script.py: generation and script execution split into generate() and run_serializer(); A1 behaviour unchanged.
- llm.py: server_session takes extra environment for the critic's workspace roots.
- config.toml: [critic] policy, rounds, fix_attempts.
- qa_delta.py: per-revision fixed/regressed gold triples by addressed rule.
- score.py variants() and score_run() know a1_critic.

Runs scored under this entry: qa-det-v1 (policy disabled), qa-llm-v1 (policy manual).
